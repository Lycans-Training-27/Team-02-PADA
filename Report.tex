\subsection{Electrical and Avionics Design}

The electrical architecture of the PADA aircraft is designed to deliver regulated, reliable power to high-draw propulsion components and sensitive digital avionics while maintaining strict weight and safety margins.

\subsubsection{Component Selection and Power Budget}

A comprehensive power analysis was conducted to size the power source and ensure system stability across nominal cruise and peak throttle flight regimes.

\textbf{Battery Endurance Calculation:}
Assuming a continuous nominal cruise draw of $9.38\,\text{A}$ on a $1300\,\text{mAh}$ battery, total nominal operational flight time $t_{\text{flight}}$ is calculated as:
\begin{equation}
    t_{\text{flight}} = \frac{1.3\,\text{Ah} \times 0.80 \text{ (usable capacity)}}{9.38\,\text{A}} \times 60\,\text{min/hr} \approx 6.65\,\text{minutes}
\end{equation}
This comfortably satisfies the $3\text{--}5$ minute mission profile with a $20\%$ capacity safety buffer.

\subsubsection{Wiring, Connectors, Protection, and Electrical Safety}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{High-Current Path:} Primary power from the 3S LiPo to the ESC uses 14 AWG silicone wire rated for up to $40\,\text{A}$ continuous, terminated with an **XT60 anti-spark connector** serving as the master physical arming switch.
    \item \textbf{Low-Voltage Signal Path:} Avionics and servo communication lines utilize 26 AWG twisted-pair wiring to reduce high-frequency electromagnetic interference (EMI) generated by motor switching.
    \item \textbf{Voltage Regulation \& Protection:} Power to the Pixhawk, sensors, and servos is stepped down to $5.0\,\text{V DC}$ using the 30A ESC's integrated linear Battery Eliminator Circuit (BEC). An inline fuse and capacitor bank on the main power distribution board suppress inductive voltage spikes during high-acceleration maneuvers.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Component Placement and Integration}

To maintain dynamic balance and aerodynamic stability, hardware components are arranged within the internal fuselage cavity as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Center of Gravity (CG) Alignment:} The heaviest components—the 3S LiPo battery ($115\,\text{g}$) and Pixhawk FC ($35\,\text{g}$)—are placed directly over the calculated aircraft CG ($25\text{--}30\%$ mean aerodynamic chord) on a sliding tray to allow fine trim adjustments.
    \item \textbf{Vibration Isolation:} The Pixhawk flight controller is mounted on anti-vibration damping foam to prevent high-frequency motor vibrations from degrading IMU sensor fusion performance.
    \item \textbf{EMI Separation:} The NEO-M8N GPS/compass module is mounted externally on a elevated $10\,\text{cm}$ carbon mast clear of motor leads, battery current paths, and high-frequency telemetry antennas to prevent magnetic interference.
\end{itemize}
