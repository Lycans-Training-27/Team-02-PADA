\subsection{Mission Requirements and Design Constraints}
Mission Objective is to design a small unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) resembling the Powered Autonomous Delivery Aircraft, aka PADA. The aim of the project is to simulate both aircraft design
and manufacturing processes, which will follow when the season begins. Designing the aircraft
means working on the wing, tail, fuselage, and electrical system.

\subsubsection{Electrical System Architecture and Safety}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Power Distribution:} A 3S LiPo battery supplies main battery voltage directly to the 30A ESC. The integrated Battery Eliminator Circuit (BEC) regulates power down to $5\,\text{V DC}$ to safely drive the flight controller, RC receiver, and onboard servos.
    \item \textbf{Autopilot \& ROS 2 Integration:} The Pixhawk 2.4.8 runs ArduPilot firmware and communicates with the companion computer via a TELEM serial port. MAVROS bridges TELEM commands directly to ROS 2 nodes for autonomous mission execution and mode switching.
    \item \textbf{Electrical Safety:} A high-current XT60 main connector serves as the master power switch. All signal lines utilize shielded twisted-pair wiring to prevent electromagnetic interference (EMI) from high-current motor leads.
\end{itemize}
